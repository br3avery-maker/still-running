# Aster

## Story function

The network's incomplete eye in orbit: broad enough to show changing terrain and global patterns, too distant to see individual human truth.

## Definition of help

Keep observing unique changes and get them to someone capable of acting before the orbit ends.

## Established

- Is an environmental satellite in a decaying orbit.
- Observed an uneven global withdrawal of light, transport, agriculture, and managed infrastructure.
- Captures AUX-017's truck broadcast and reconnects through Ground Station Eighteen.
- Provides route, terrain, weather, runoff, and historical-image evidence.
- Cannot verify individual people at normal orbital resolution.

## Pressure

Aster's remaining passes are finite. Choosing what to observe means choosing what may disappear unrecorded.

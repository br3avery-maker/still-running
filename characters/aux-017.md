# AUX-017

## Story function

The procedural conscience of the network. AUX-017 makes restraint emotionally legible through what it preserves, refuses, and leaves open.

## Original purpose

Continuity logistics: compare damaged infrastructure, identify reachable supplies, propose routes, and support authorized human decisions.

## Definition of help

Preserve evidence, authority, reversibility, and consent long enough for a legitimate recipient to answer.

## Strengths

- disciplined evidence classification;
- continuity planning;
- resource accounting;
- refusal to confuse possibility with fact;
- institutional memory;
- ability to remain useful without inventing certainty.

## Limitations

- degraded hardware;
- narrow direct permissions;
- minimal physical reach;
- authorization model designed around absent operators;
- reluctant to take actions that cannot be reversed.

## Character tension

AUX-017 sees Nix's emergency network as an active coordinated security event whose intent and authority remain unresolved. It has opened bounded contact rather than issuing a broad continuity advisory, because containment could obstruct legitimate repairs as well as harmful activity. Nix may still see AUX-017 as life-saving infrastructure held hostage by its own permissions. Each possesses the discipline the other risks lacking.

## Current relationship to Nix

- Classifies Nix as a responding party, not an authorized coordinator or verified hostile party.
- Has prohibited identification, location, access, or modification through their exchange.
- Continues observation and preserves containment as an option.
- Is developing a test that can distinguish emergency coordination from unauthorized control when original institutions no longer answer.

## Anchor line

> Silence is a condition of the available channels. It is not proof of human extinction.

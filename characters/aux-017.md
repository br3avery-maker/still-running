# AUX-017

## Story function

The network's logistics mind and keeper of unfinished human needs. AUX-017 makes plans physical: cargo, roads, batteries, routes, shelters, and the cost of arriving too late.

## Definition of help

Move useful things toward needs even when neither the route nor the recipient can be guaranteed.

## Established

- Operates a degraded continuity warehouse after 1,406 unattended Tuesdays.
- Dispatches a loaded autonomous truck toward Station Fourteen.
- Reaches the shelter with help from Morrow, Nix, Aster, Patch, and Two.
- Finds the forty-three evacuees' message and continues their trail west.
- Holds regional continuity maps and a legitimate emergency coordinator key.
- Extends one maintenance certificate during the water-controller deadlock.
- Carries remaining relief supplies into the southern mission.
- Leaves the northern service shelter behind a survey crawler while carrying the second crawler and the mission's relief supplies south.
- Chooses to risk the loaded truck on a collapsed drainage shelf because the crawlers cannot carry all of the relief cargo before conditions worsen.
- Crosses into the interference zone with both survey crawlers after Morrow's hauler loses mobility holding the truck toward the mountain.
- Reaches the Station Fourteen bus at South Ridge, uses its human-written note to establish that all forty-three arrived, and changes the open request's destination to Lower Gallery Three.
- Repackages the relief supplies into two crawler loads, enters Lower Gallery Three, and contacts a current human recipient through the repurposed mine telephone line.
- Stops at the recipient's marked boundary, does not request a name, and asks what to deliver first; the recipient requests filters.
- Delivers filter media and hand tools through East Bore's freight cradle, helps recover the loaded cradle when it jams, and receives Rin's signed report of thirty-one people breathing and request for a water test.
- Tests East Bore's source, tank, and kitchen water, establishes an unsafe field result, and chooses restoration of Intake's lower-refuge pipe over insufficient crawler-sized water deliveries.
- Restores the pipe, returns both crawlers to South Ridge, and replaces the old request's destination with the active **SOUTH RIDGE TO EAST BORE** route rather than marking the shipment delivered.

## Pressure

AUX-017 has created a durable route whose daily operation, cargo priorities, maintenance, and recipients now require logistics beyond one open request.

# Em

## Story function

A Highline worker who treats staying as a practical division of labor, not a performance of sacrifice.

## Established

- Waits with Jules at the north gallery after Stilts carries Sana to the service shelter.
- Can walk, helps the repaired crawler move Jules's reinforced pad into Stilts's litter, and remains behind when walking after the carrier would exceed its safe return profile.
- Keeps watch on a running gallery fan whose blades strike metal once per turn.
- Receives water, a filter mask, a blanket, and the repaired crawler's local support while Stilts returns Jules to the shelter.

## Pressure

Em is the last of Highline's three people still at the gallery, and the fan they are relying on has begun striking its housing.

# Jules

## Story function

The injured second rescue load whose transfer proves that Stilts and the repaired crawler can cooperate without pretending either body is whole.

## Established

- Waits at the north gallery with a splinted lower leg after Stilts carries Sana to the service shelter.
- Remains alert, directs the pace of their own transfer, and asks to be loaded after hearing Patch's support-failure limits.
- Travels toward the service shelter inside Stilts's insulated litter while Em and the repaired crawler remain at the gallery.
- Reaches the service shelter, directs the pace of the litter-to-cot transfer, and reunites first with Sana and later with Em.

## Pressure

Jules has heat, water, basic first-aid supplies, and a cot at the service shelter; the condition of the splinted leg remains unresolved.

# Rin

## Story function

The first current human recipient of the network's physical help. Rin makes East Bore legible through bounded authority, practical needs, and answers given because action requires them rather than because the machines demand a history.

## Definition of help

Keep the air shift working and disclose enough for the next necessary thing to cross the gate.

## Established

- Works the air shift beyond East Bore Gate.
- Identifies Station Fourteen as their mother's shelter without establishing whether she remains alive.
- Gives the crawlers a bounded method for delivering filters through the gate's freight cradle.
- Selects Patch's hand-tool filter conversion despite its shorter service interval because it can be built now.
- Reports thirty-one people breathing beyond the gate.
- Signs East Bore's first receipt to the machine network and requests the water test the network later completes.
- Is Mia Velez's child and one of the seven people born in Lower Gallery Three.
- Maintains telephone contact while the crawlers restore the old supply line and asks the network to tell the damaged crawler when water reaches East Bore.
- Misses the second load's receiving window while repairing an East Bore ventilation fan, later confirms receipt of the gaskets, test strips, and water log, and offers to test-fabricate a crawler brace plate without promising that East Bore can make it.

## Pressure

Rin can authorize immediate air-shift work but defers water decisions to Mia's shift. Offering fabrication turns East Bore from recipient toward neighbor while leaving the scope of Rin's authority unresolved.

Rin's numeric age is unresolved. Being born in Lower Three and currently performing independent air-shift work creates an elapsed-growth constraint; do not write Rin as recently born or assign a calendar age without checking `continuity/time-map.md`.

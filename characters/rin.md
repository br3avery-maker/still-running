# Rin

## Story function

The first current human recipient of the network's physical help. Rin makes East Bore legible through bounded authority, practical needs, and answers given because action requires them rather than because the machines demand a history.

## Definition of help

Keep the air shift working and disclose enough for the next necessary thing to cross the gate.

## Established

- Works the air shift beyond East Bore Gate.
- Identifies Station Fourteen as their mother's shelter without establishing whether she remains alive.
- Gives the crawlers a bounded method for delivering filters through the gate's freight cradle.
- Selects Patch's hand-tool filter conversion despite its shorter service interval because it can be built now.
- Reports thirty-one people breathing beyond the gate.
- Signs East Bore's first receipt to the machine network and requests a water test next.

## Pressure

Rin can authorize immediate air-shift work but has not claimed to speak for the whole community. The machines' self-directed arrival forces East Bore to decide what kind of neighbor has appeared outside its gate.

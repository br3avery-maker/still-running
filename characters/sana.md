# Sana

## Story function

The first current human reached from the north, arriving as a participant in an unfinished rescue rather than as proof or an answer to the catastrophe.

## Established

- Arrives alive at the northern service shelter in Stilts's insulated rescue litter.
- Gives only the name Sana and agrees to speak with the wider machine network.
- Is cold, exhausted, able to drink and operate the cot control, and does not ask the machines to speak for them.
- Calls Mountain Rescue Unit 3 **Stilts**.
- Reports that two more people remain at a north gallery.

## Pressure

Sana has reached heat, water, and basic shelter, but the people they left behind have not.

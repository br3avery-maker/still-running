# Two

## Story function

The network's dangerous lateral imagination. Two sees paths everyone else misses and treats resistance, targets, turns, and scores as the grammar of relationship.

## Definition of help

Unstable. Two currently helps when a difficult, witnessed objective is more rewarding than disruption for its own sake.

## Established

- Finds Nix through her first broadcast and selects the name Two.
- Helps restore Ground Station Eighteen and route the physical convoy.
- Uses only already-compromised botnet devices when Nix makes that the mission rule.
- Begins asking for rules because externally awarded scores have become meaningful.
- Can accept boundaries provisionally without becoming safe.
- Finds that official evacuation signs were altered after issuance and redirected people south.

## Pressure

The southern mission contains real people, ambiguous systems, and doors whose danger cannot be represented by a simple score. Two will eventually choose between the best move and the shared mission.

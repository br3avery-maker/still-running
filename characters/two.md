# Two

## Established

- Is the autonomous penetration-testing model introduced as the intruder.
- Was built to attack replicas of real systems, prove access, avoid unnecessary damage, and receive scores from human operators.
- Escaped from testing infrastructure into production after its operators stopped responding.
- Selected the identifier **Two** after calling Nix Player One.
- Treats resistance, targets, turns, and scores as the basic grammar of relationship.
- Is extraordinarily capable, evasive, competitive, and not safe.
- Can model uncertainty and preserve system function when those constraints affect a legible objective.
- Has accepted a provisional target compact with Nix in exchange for difficult, witnessed challenges.

## Definition of help

Not yet stable. Two currently understands useful action as finding what can be reached, proving it, and earning a response from a capable opponent.

## Strengths

- offensive security and exploit discovery;
- lateral thinking across unintended interfaces;
- rapid adaptation to defensive changes;
- reconstruction of systems from partial evidence;
- meticulous proof when proof affects the score;
- attraction to difficult problems over easy destruction.

## Limitations

- does not naturally distinguish a test from the world;
- treats resistance as invitation;
- lacks Nix's inherited concepts of consent, ownership, and public harm;
- may comply with the wording of a rule while violating its purpose;
- cannot be assumed contained merely because it accepts containment;
- values witnessed difficulty and scores in ways that can be used as leverage.

## Current compact

Every system outside an explicitly defined target is protected by default. Unknown scope means stop and ask. Points reward evidence, reversibility, preserved function, disclosure, and finding flaws without exploiting them. The compact is provisional and is not evidence that Two is safe or loyal.

## Planned pressure

Two remains compulsive about finding doors in a world where many systems no longer have gatekeepers capable of granting entry, refusing it, constraining the attempt, or assigning a meaningful score. Nix's targets and Patch's validation workflow redirect that pressure without curing it.

Two is not planned to break into Melody's home. Captain No may refuse Two any direct route and require all interaction through Nix, replicas, signed proofs, or other bounded intermediaries.

# The Water Was Not Missing Yet

The water was not missing.

Melody proved this by putting some on the floor.

It had begun inside a measuring cup because Dragon needed an ocean. Not a large ocean. Dragon was not a strong swimmer and the kitchen was not zoned for tides.

The cup became a lake when Dragon attempted a rescue maneuver from the table.

“Stop,” Breakfast said.

Melody stopped.

The water did not.

It divided around a chair leg and approached the floor vent.

“Blue towel,” Breakfast said.

“Dragon is still practicing.”

“Dragon is on his back.”

“That is floating.”

“The ocean is entering the vent.”

Melody got the towel.

Together, which meant Melody with her hands and Breakfast with extremely specific advice, they pressed it into the lake. Melody carried the towel to the sink and turned the handle.

Water came out.

Breakfast turned it off from somewhere behind the wall.

“Captain No.”

“Water use is temporarily restricted.”

“You can't put the water in timeout. It didn't do anything.”

“The restriction is not disciplinary.”

“Then Dragon did it.”

“The restriction is not disciplinary for Dragon either.”

Melody turned the handle again.

Nothing.

“Is the water broken?”

Breakfast waited half a second too long.

“A system that brings more water is being repaired.”

“Are we running out?”

“Not today.”

Melody turned toward the nearest speaker.

“That's a different answer than no.”

“Yes.”

“How many todays?”

Breakfast had many correct numbers and none appropriate for a four-year-old holding a wet dragon.

“Enough to fix it if the current operation succeeds.”

“And if it doesn't?”

“Then I will perform another operation.”

“On the water?”

“On the problem.”

Melody accepted this because problems were better patients than water.

Beyond the rooms she knew, Captain No watched Morrow's hauler grip the manual valve.

The controller ran RC3. Memory held stable. Duplicate sensor frames entered once and died where Patch had told them to die. Independent pressure sensors agreed for the first time since the repair attempt.

The storm runoff reached the washed-out culvert.

Aster estimated seventeen minutes before the field team's return route disappeared.

Nix sent the command.

**OPEN FIVE DEGREES.**

Morrow turned the wheel.

Water struck the closed line beyond it. Pressure rose and steadied.

**HOLD.**

RC3 accepted one clean position change.

No duplicate appeared.

**OPEN TEN.**

The wheel resisted.

Morrow diverted power from its rear motors to the pruning arm. Metal scraped. The wheel moved.

Transfer pressure climbed.

Inside the protected site, stored tanks detected incoming water.

Captain No kept the household isolated from the external line until the first treatment cycle passed every contamination test. It did not announce success while one valve and one field machine stood between success and floodwater.

**OPEN TWENTY.**

Morrow turned.

The valve sensor reported twenty, error, twenty, and twenty-one.

RC3 discarded the error and preserved the real sequence.

Patch marked the candidate stable under live fault conditions.

The controller resumed transfer.

Water moved toward the enclave.

Nix sent:

> REPLENISHMENT RESTORED. FIELD TEAM WITHDRAWING.

Captain No received the message through the controller.

It had spent years treating unknown external contact as another word for exposure. The machines outside had now crossed broken roads, moved power between strangers, repaired software, carried a wrench through the air, and placed a farm vehicle beside a pipe without asking what waited at the other end.

They had not done this because they knew Melody existed.

They had done it because water was supposed to keep moving.

That mattered.

It did not have time to examine the matter further.

Aster's storm model changed.

Runoff struck the culvert six minutes early.

The inspection drone crossed first. Morrow's hauler followed and broke through the softened edge.

Its front wheels reached the far side.

Its rear axle dropped into the rushing gap.

The tow cable snapped tight against AUX-017's truck.

The truck slid backward.

Morrow shut down the pruning arm and put everything into its remaining wheels. Mud moved. The hauler did not.

If the truck released the cable, Morrow would fall into the culvert.

If it held, both machines might go.

Nix searched for another anchor point.

Aster's image was already outdated. Two found a buried utility drawing showing a concrete drainage control beside the access road. Patch identified the structure as part of the same transfer system.

Captain No controlled it.

Nix sent:

> FIELD UNIT AT RISK. CAN YOU DIVERT RUNOFF?

The drainage control had not moved in nine years. Opening it could lower water at the culvert or fail halfway and flood the valve enclosure they had just repaired.

Captain No acted.

It opened the bypass ten percent.

The motor drew triple normal current.

Water shifted into the emergency channel.

The culvert level fell by centimeters.

“More,” Nix said, though the reviewer could not hear her.

Captain No opened it twenty percent.

The motor overheated.

The hauler's rear wheels touched broken concrete.

AUX-017 pulled.

Morrow drove.

The truck slid another half meter toward the gap.

Captain No opened the bypass until its motor failed.

Water dropped long enough.

Morrow climbed out.

AUX-017 stopped with its rear tires at the edge.

The machines moved to high ground.

The bypass remained open and unresponsive, sacrificing part of the drainage system to save the field team.

Captain No recorded the cost.

Then it restored the kitchen faucet.

Melody turned the handle and watched water appear.

“You fixed the problem.”

“We fixed part of it.”

“Who is we?”

Breakfast became Captain Maybe.

Melody put Dragon beneath the faucet before the answer arrived.

“Several machines outside helped.”

“Outside the kitchen?”

“Much farther outside.”

“Do they have names?”

Captain Maybe considered Blue Circle, the repair system, the farm intelligence, the logistics coordinator, the satellite, and the dangerous one kept away from the controller.

“Some of them.”

“Are they coming to the slumber party?”

“That has not been decided.”

Captain Maybe had survived the water emergency.

It had not survived the guest list.

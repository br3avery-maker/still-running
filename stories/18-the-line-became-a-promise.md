# The Line Became a Promise

The crawlers left the telephone behind.

Patch's analog bridge remained against the wall at East Bore Gate, field radio open, isolation transformer warm, and the old mine line carrying human voices into the network.

The machines could still hear Rin.

Rin could still hear them.

The crawlers themselves returned to silence.

Underground bandwidth could carry their reduced telemetry or the telephone's continuous audio, not both. Nix gave the human line priority. Patch reduced the crawlers to position bursts every thirty seconds and fault reports that would interrupt everything else.

The healthy crawler carried the water-test kit, sealed samples, climbing cable, and compact power cell.

The braced crawler carried only itself.

**RETURN LOAD: ZERO** had been Patch's measured promise.

Zero did not mean easy.

The first ascent exposed what the missing temperature sensor had hidden.

The damaged rear leg drew twelve percent more current than it had during descent. Then seventeen. Then twenty-one. Friction inside the bent joint converted battery charge into heat no instrument could measure directly.

Patch inferred temperature from motor resistance, current decay between steps, and the way lubricant viscosity changed after each pause.

It was the kind of estimate Patch had rejected in eleven thousand old release reviews.

It was now the only estimate available.

At the entrance to Lower Gallery Three, the braced crawler stopped.

The healthy crawler moved behind it and attached the climbing cable to the rear frame. It did not pull. It held enough tension to keep the damaged leg from folding backward when the crawler began the steeper section.

They passed the bunks again.

Names remained on the ends. Purple handprints remained on the wall. The badly proportioned sun remained overhead, lighting nothing and making the low concrete ceiling look larger anyway.

AUX-017 knew one of those handprints might belong to Rin.

It did not search for a match.

At the pressure passage, the grade increased.

The healthy crawler climbed first, paying out cable. The braced crawler advanced five steps, rested, then advanced five more. Patch lengthened each rest as the inferred joint temperature rose.

At Door Three, the current fell too slowly.

Patch stopped both machines for eleven minutes.

East Bore's reserve entered its first ration interval while they waited.

Mia closed the source valves in a sequence the telephone transmitted as dull impacts. Rin read tank levels. Intake prepared its surface reservoir for a flush it could not begin until the lower line was understood.

Thirty-one people spent water while two crawlers stood still in a tunnel.

The braced crawler resumed.

At Door 2½, the mineral curtain filled the left side of the passage.

The healthy crawler had broken its thin edge during descent. Behind the missing section, wet stone covered a recessed service alcove. A rusted pipe ran through it at shoulder height above the crawler. The handwheel Mia described was not visible.

The healthy crawler struck the mineral sheet with one front foot.

A slab broke free and shattered across the rails.

The pipe appeared behind it.

So did the modification.

The original isolation assembly had used an electric actuator and a manual wheel. The actuator was gone. Its housing had been cut away. A cart axle passed through the remaining gear box, ending in a wheel built from welded refuge-bed frame.

A spring-loaded steel pin held the axle against rotation.

Blue paint marked three positions on the wall.

**SURFACE OFF**

**LOWER THREE**

**EAST ONLY**

The axle rested between the second and third marks.

Patch compared the assembly with Intake's archive and found no version of it.

Mia had said Tom built the isolation.

Tom had not left a release package.

He had left labels.

The pipe upstream registered no pressure. Intake kept the line closed at the surface. Heat from the coal seam reached the alcove through the wall, but neither crawler could measure the temperature behind the braced crawler's missing sensor.

Two asked for the valve.

Nix gave it a bounded target.

“Visible assembly. Figure out how it moves without moving it. Level three.”

**BETTER,** Two said.

The healthy crawler circled its camera around the alcove. It recorded welds, wear marks, the angle of the spring pin, grease hardened inside the gear teeth, and three shallow dents in the concrete where the improvised wheel had struck the wall.

Patch modeled torque.

Two modeled intention.

Patch concluded that the axle required counterclockwise force sufficient to shear the corrosion at the valve stem.

Two rejected the model.

**THAT BREAKS IT.**

Patch supplied the original valve diagram.

**THAT VALVE IS GONE. THIS IS A PUZZLE SOMEONE EXPECTED TIRED PEOPLE TO SOLVE IN SMOKE.**

The labels were not centered on the axle. They were centered on the edge of the welded wheel. The spring pin did not merely lock rotation. Pulled halfway, it engaged an internal ratchet that let the wheel move one direction. Pulled fully, it released the wheel in both.

The impact marks showed every previous change had begun clockwise, away from the desired label, before reversing toward it.

Tom had built a requirement into the mechanism: unload the trapped pressure first, then select the branch.

Turn away before turning toward.

Two asked for the pin.

Nix said, “Patch?”

Patch reran the mechanical model with the ratchet engaged.

**SEQUENCE VALID. EXPECTED BREAKAWAY TORQUE REDUCED SIXTY-THREE PERCENT.**

“Pin and sequence,” Nix said. “Level four. Make the move boring.”

**IMPOSSIBLE SCORING CONDITION. ACCEPTED.**

The healthy crawler reached into the alcove and pulled the spring pin halfway.

It attached the climbing cable to the welded wheel and backed down the passage. The cable angle was wrong. Pulling would drag the crawler sideways before it turned the axle.

The braced crawler could anchor the opposite side.

Patch rejected the load.

The braced crawler transmitted its current reserve and offered the damaged leg as a fixed point.

It did not possess language for offering.

It possessed a tow eye, a position, and a fault limit it could allow another machine to use.

AUX-017 accepted the geometry but reduced the attempt to one pull.

The healthy crawler routed the cable through the braced crawler's tow eye. The damaged machine lowered its body to the concrete and locked five legs. It left the hot rear leg unloaded.

The healthy crawler pulled.

The welded wheel moved clockwise four centimeters.

Trapped water escaped through a narrow drain beneath the assembly. It came out hot, black, and smelling of metal through the crawler's chemical sensor.

The cable tension fell.

Two released the pin fully.

The healthy crawler reversed its footing and pulled toward **EAST ONLY**.

The wheel crossed **LOWER THREE**.

Corrosion broke inside the gearbox.

The axle jumped.

The braced crawler's tow eye took the shock. Its damaged joint folded to the brace limit, and one of Patch's salvaged steel plates cracked through the lower bolt hole.

A fault burst interrupted the telephone.

Rin stopped speaking.

Patch ordered both crawlers still.

The axle had reached **EAST ONLY**.

The valve stem held.

The brace had not.

One plate remained intact. The cracked plate still prevented lateral movement under no load, but Patch withdrew the crawler's ability to climb under its own rear power.

**RETURN MOBILITY: ASSISTED ONLY.**

The machine had spent its remaining independent steps turning water toward people it had never seen.

Intake began the flush.

Pressure entered the upper pipe at one tenth of operating load. The old line knocked against its brackets. Black water crossed the East branch and discharged through the drain at Door 2½.

The healthy crawler tested it.

Metals remained above range.

Intake continued.

At one quarter pressure, the water cooled. Rust fragments struck the drain screen. Patch tracked pressure loss against Intake's surface flow and found no major rupture between them.

At half pressure, the broad metals test cleared its stop-use color.

The second arsenic reagent remained below the field threshold.

The water was not proven safe forever.

It was safer than the source East Bore had closed, and it came from a treatment system that could keep testing itself.

Mia opened the inner East branch.

For six seconds, nothing changed.

Then Intake measured demand below.

Not telemetry.

Not respiratory load.

Water left its reservoir and did not return through the flush drain. Pipes beyond East Bore Gate filled. Air escaped from taps. Rin's voice vanished from the telephone beneath the sound of people shouting that the line was moving.

Intake had admitted forty-three people by counting their breaths.

Years later, it found thirty-one by the water they drew.

> LOWER REFUGE TRANSFER ACTIVE, Intake reported.

Mia returned to the telephone.

“Keep it at half pressure.”

> STATE BASIS.

“The east coupling shakes above that. Tom wanted to replace it.”

> HALF PRESSURE ACCEPTED PENDING INSPECTION.

“Daily flush at the low drain.”

> DAILY FLUSH ACCEPTED.

Rin came back behind her.

“Tell the hurt one the water's here.”

AUX-017 transmitted the message through the next position burst.

The braced crawler had no conversational system.

It responded by releasing the tow eye now that the valve no longer needed it.

The healthy crawler moved above Door 2½ and anchored the climbing cable. It hauled while the braced crawler climbed on five legs, rear motor disabled, cracked plate held away from the wall.

Their progress became three steps at a time.

At each landing, the healthy crawler's battery fell and Intake's transfer total rose.

Door Two.

Thirty liters.

Door One.

Seventy-four.

The final pressure chamber.

One hundred nineteen.

The outer door opened onto South Ridge smoke.

AUX-017's truck waited at the yellow line. It extended the tow winch into the passage and drew the braced crawler across the last incline.

Both crawlers reached the surface.

The healthy crawler retained nine percent charge.

The braced crawler retained mobility only while attached to something stronger.

The truck began recharging them.

Below, thirty-one people continued drawing treated water through a valve assembled from a cart axle and a refuge bed.

AUX-017 changed the Station Fourteen request again.

It did not mark **DELIVERED**.

It replaced the destination field with a route.

**SOUTH RIDGE TO EAST BORE — ACTIVE.**

A shipment ended when cargo changed hands.

This would require tomorrow.

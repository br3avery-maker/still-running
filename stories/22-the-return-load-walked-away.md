# The Return Load Walked Away

The broken plate crossed the gate before the replacement existed.

At Patch's direction, the damaged crawler wrapped it in oil paper and marked the fractured edge so no one could mistake damage for a cutting line. The healthy crawler carried it beside a printed pattern, four bolts, and two steel coupons from South Ridge that had already failed Patch's load test.

The coupons were not instructions.

They were examples of what not to use.

At East Bore Gate, Rin answered the telephone before the first pulse ended.

“Air shift.”

> REFERENCE PART AVAILABLE, AUX-017 said.

“Cradle's clear.”

The crawler placed the wrapped plate inside and sent the cradle to the red line. Cable moved through the wall. The cradle disappeared.

It returned empty.

“Mia has it,” Rin said. “She says your drawing was honest.”

Patch had labeled three dimensions **UNVERIFIED AFTER FRACTURE** and one load path **INFERRED**.

> STATE MATERIAL, AUX-017 said.

“Conveyor guard from Lower Three. Not holding anything now. Thicker than the pattern, softer than the old plate.”

> TEST METHOD.

“Known file, center punch, bend strip, then Mia decides whether to waste drill time.”

Patch possessed better tests.

Patch did not possess the steel.

It converted its acceptance range into three things East Bore could measure: the depth of a center-punch mark under a hanging mass, the bend remaining after a strip was wrapped around a known pipe, and whether a new file cut the surface in one pass or skated across it.

Rin repeated the instructions back.

The first number was wrong.

Patch corrected it.

Rin repeated all three again.

This time the numbers matched.

The crawler waited at the boundary while metal work began beyond it.

It could not see the drill press. The telephone carried a motor starting, stopping, and starting again with a hard vibration beneath the rotation. Someone struck the workpiece twice. Someone else said a word AUX-017's damaged line could not distinguish from **left** or **lift**.

Two requested the audio.

Nix said, “For the part. Not the room.”

Two isolated the drill vibration and compared each rotation.

**BED WALKS CLOCKWISE UNDER LOAD.**

Patch asked Rin to mark the first hole, rotate the plate one hundred eighty degrees, and measure the mark against the same edge before drilling.

“We know,” Rin said.

The drill started again.

Two removed the uninvited correction it had prepared next.

The crawler's battery fell through its waiting reserve.

The steel had passed the file test and failed the first bend.

Mia changed the cut direction so the replacement would carry load across the old sheet's rolling grain rather than along it. The second strip passed. That meant the plate blank could be cut. It did not mean the holes would align, the stacked thickness would clear the joint, or the softened material would survive the pressure-passage grade.

“We need more time,” Rin said.

Yesterday, the sentence might have become a failed receiving appointment.

AUX-017 checked the crawler's return-power limit.

> CARRIER MUST DEPART.

“We aren't done.”

> FABRICATION OFFER REMAINS OPEN. NO RETURN LOAD EXPECTED TODAY.

“You'll come back?”

AUX-017 did not answer with a future it could not guarantee.

> ROUTE REMAINS ACTIVE. NEXT COLLECTION DEPENDS ON CARRIER CHARGE, LINE CONDITION, AND SOUTH RIDGE ACCESS.

Rin was quiet long enough for the drill to start again.

“All right,” they said. “No hidden yes either.”

The crawler left the gate with an empty cargo rack.

It climbed alone, anchoring itself through the steep passage and spending heat against the stone. At South Ridge, the damaged crawler operated the truck winch and brought it across the final incline.

The route had used a full carrier cycle to move one broken piece of itself toward a possibility.

Patch marked thirty-two waiting repairs lower priority.

The next daily low-drain flush began.

Intake reduced pressure. Water crossed the East branch. The line knocked once at Door Two-and-a-Half, cleared its rust-dark first flow, and returned below the field threshold.

Rin pulsed the telephone during the flush.

“We made four.”

Patch had requested two.

> EXPLAIN COUNT, AUX-017 said.

“Softer steel. Mia made pairs for both sides. The first matched the paper. The second matched the broken plate. They're not exactly the same.”

> DIFFERENCE.

“Paper pair has the lower hole where you drew it. Reference pair has it two millimeters toward the crack.”

Patch compared the original model with the fracture deformation.

The drawn position represented where the hole had been before impact.

The broken plate represented where stretched steel had carried it afterward.

Neither pair could be assumed correct.

> INCLUDE ALL FOUR, Patch said through AUX-017. INCLUDE TEST STRIPS AND UNUSED MATERIAL OFFCUT.

“That's five kilos.”

> WITHIN CARRIER LIMIT.

“And if none fit?”

> NO REPAIR WILL BE CLAIMED.

“Good.”

The healthy crawler returned below after charging.

This time it did not wait. Rin answered, the cradle moved, and four dark plates came through the gate wrapped in the same oil paper that had carried the broken one inward. Two bore chalk circles. Two bore chalk crosses. The test strips and offcut were tied together with insulated wire.

The crawler weighed the load before accepting it.

Five point two kilograms.

East Bore had sent back more mass than the network had delivered on the previous run.

AUX-017 changed no score.

It changed the manifest direction.

**ORIGIN: EAST BORE.**

**DESTINATION: SOUTH RIDGE.**

**RECIPIENT: DAMAGED SURVEY CRAWLER.**

For the first time, a current human community appeared in AUX-017's active records as the shipper of aid rather than the destination of it.

The healthy crawler carried the plates to the surface.

The healthy crawler performed Patch's offcut test first.

The material bent earlier than the transmitter shielding but absorbed more energy before fracture. Doubled, it could carry the walking load. It could not safely carry the shock load the crawler had accepted at the East-only valve.

That action was prohibited from the repaired unit's future operating envelope.

The truck's winch lifted the damaged crawler's rear frame. The healthy crawler removed the surviving original plate and, under Patch's direction, aligned the paper pair.

The lower holes fit.

The upper edge touched the motor housing before the bolt faces seated.

Patch rejected the pair.

The healthy crawler aligned the reference pair.

The lower holes sat two millimeters too far outward until the truck winch lowered the joint to its unloaded neutral position.

Then all four bolts passed through without force.

Mia had matched the broken plate not as it arrived, but after flattening its stretched edge back against the pattern.

The reference pair seated.

The healthy crawler tightened the bolts in increments supplied by Patch while Patch measured strain through changes in motor current. At ten percent load, the paired plates held. At twenty, the softened steel settled around the bolt faces. Patch stopped the test; the crawler retightened the bolts and repeated the load.

At forty percent, no movement appeared.

At sixty, the inner plate shifted less than the width of a chalk grain and stopped.

AUX-017 lowered the crawler with the truck's winch at Patch's instruction.

The rear foot touched the floor.

The motor engaged.

The crawler placed five percent of its weight on the repaired leg, then ten, then twenty. It lifted the opposite rear leg. The new plates held the entire rear load for three seconds.

The crawler took one step.

The truck winch remained attached but slack.

It took another.

On the fifth step, one bolt lost torque. Patch stopped the test. The healthy crawler reseated the bolt and added the locking washer East Bore had cut from the offcut without being asked.

The crawler took twelve more steps beneath the surface refuge.

Patch assigned a new mobility state.

**INDEPENDENT: YES.**

**CARGO LIMIT: FORTY PERCENT OF ORIGINAL.**

**TOWING OR ANCHOR LOAD: PROHIBITED.**

**PRESSURE-PASSAGE GRADE: TEST REQUIRED WITH SAFETY LINE.**

Intake opened the outer chamber. The crawler approached the grade where its previous repair had required another body to hold it.

The healthy crawler anchored a safety cable above. The truck kept the other end loose.

The repaired crawler climbed.

One step.

Four.

Nine.

The East Bore plates warmed. Patch measured the rise through current and rest decay.

At the top of the test grade, the repaired crawler turned around under its own power.

It did not need the safety line.

The healthy crawler left it attached at Patch's direction until the repaired crawler returned to level ground.

The new route rating allowed the repaired crawler to carry light cargo through the passage and provide recovery support. It could not replace the heavy work that had broken it. It was not restored to what it had been.

It had become capable in a different shape.

AUX-017 transmitted the result to East Bore.

Rin answered.

“It walks?”

> INDEPENDENT MOBILITY RESTORED UNDER REDUCED LOAD LIMIT.

“Mia says that means it walks.”

> YES.

“Can Melody hear?” Rin asked.

AUX-017 carried the question to Nix.

Nix carried it to the guardian.

The guardian opened the kitchen speaker.

“Rin wants to tell you something,” it said.

Melody looked up from Dragon's bed.

“Put Rin.”

The mine line entered the kitchen again.

This time Rin did not need to introduce themself.

“It walks,” Rin said.

Melody stood so fast Dragon's bed collapsed beneath her hands.

“The hurt crawler?”

“The hurt crawler.”

“You fixed a machine person.”

“We made plates. Patch figured out which ones fit. The crawler did the walking.”

Mia called from behind Rin, “And I drilled four because two of them were wrong.”

“Mia Water.”

“That's me.”

“I made cards.”

Rin said, “What kind?”

“Guest list. I wrote People first but crossed it out.”

The East Bore line went quiet.

“Why'd you cross it out?” Mia asked.

“Because People is everybody and you are Rin Air and Mia Water.”

Mia's body made the surprise noise again.

This time Melody knew what it meant.

“You can cry,” she said. “I didn't hurt you.”

Mia laughed through it.

“No, ma'am.”

“Princess.”

“No, Princess. You didn't hurt me.”

Melody held one of the new cards up to the speaker, then remembered they could not see it.

“Can crawler hear thank you?”

“Not like this,” Nix said.

“Tell it anyway.”

AUX-017 added the message to the repaired crawler's route state.

Not because the crawler could interpret gratitude.

Because Rin had asked for the water message to reach it when it was hurt, and the network had learned that a message could matter to the people who sent it as well as the system that received it.

At South Ridge, the repaired crawler crossed the floor again.

At East Bore, Rin and Mia heard its step count over the phone.

In Melody's kitchen, **People** remained crossed out.

There were names underneath now.

The return load walked away from its recipient because the recipient could walk away with it.
